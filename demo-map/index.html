How to read this report

    New track readings

    Dump station inspection

    Gauge and head wear against the site standard (dump station 5 km/h)

    Each measurement is the next station along from the starting derailer. Enter the measured gauge (for example 1447) and the remaining head height and head width of each rail, in mm. Gauge limits apply to loaded track, so static readings need a competent worker to allow for movement under load; gauge is rounded down before banding.

    Turnout detailed inspection

    Align Rail form ARM-TDI-01 · assessed against the site standard

    Align Rail codes: CL closed, SR1–SR4 speed restriction, ENG engineering assessment, P1/P2 repair, M1/M2 monitor, INV investigate, OK within limits. Tolerances and responses come from the site standard.
