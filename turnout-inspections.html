Turnout inspections

Turnout inspections, check by check.

Every turnout is inspected against a set list of checks. Each result gets a code and photos, so you can see what needs doing first, and why.

Two levels of inspection.

Basic turnout inspection

Ten visual checks, due every 91 days:

  • Components: damaged, loose, missing or broken
  • Track geometry through the turnout
  • Bearers and fastenings
  • Ballast: condition, profile and excess
  • Rail, welds and joints: condition, wear, cracks, creep
  • Switch blades and stock rails: fit, damage, metal flow
  • Heel blocks, braces, slide plates and switch stops
  • Crossing nose and wing rails: cracks, breakouts, metal flow
  • Flangeways, check rails, spacer blocks and bolts
  • Switch operation: drive and lock bars, lever, lubrication, rollers

Detailed turnout inspection

50 checks, with measurements checked against the tolerances in your site standard, including:

  • Track gauge at the switch tip and at the crossing nose
  • Switch opening and switch blade closed gap
  • Switch blade and stock rail wear and metal flow
  • Crossing nose wear, cracks and flangeway width
  • Wing rail and check rail wear, flare and flangeways
  • Heel blocks, stops, slide plates and spacer blocks

Photos with every check.

Each check can carry its own photos, filed with the inspection. When something is found, you see exactly what the inspector saw.

Common questions.

What’s the difference between a basic and a detailed inspection?

A basic inspection is ten visual checks covering the whole turnout. A detailed inspection is 50 checks, with measurements such as gauge, switch blade gaps and crossing wear checked against your site standard.

Do you photograph what you find?

Yes. Every check can carry its own photos, so each finding comes with a picture of what was found.

How do we know what’s urgent?

Every result gets a code, from OK through planned and priority repairs and speed restrictions to closed, so the order of work is clear.

Can we get a report?

Yes. Each inspection prints or saves as a PDF, and the results stay on your map.
